# The Citadel: A Westerosi Chat Experience

**The Citadel** is an immersive, purpose-built AI chatbot completely themed around George R.R. Martin's *A Song of Ice and Fire* (and the *Game of Thrones* universe). 

Instead of wrapping a large language model in another generic, sterile messaging interface, this project brings the rich lore, aesthetics, and atmosphere of Westeros into the user experience itself. 

## Why I Chose This Topic

The core assignment asked to build an AI chatbot where the UI deeply reflects the chosen subject matter and practically demonstrates frontend thinking. 

Beyond that requirement, I chose *A Song of Ice and Fire* simply because I'm a massive fan and deeply enjoy the lore and world-building of Westeros. The fantasy genre is inherently visual and atmospheric, which gave me the perfect excuse to push beyond standard UI patterns and build a highly custom, thematic interface:

*   **Immersive Theming Engine:** I ripped out the generic "light/dark mode" toggles and built a custom styling engine centered entirely around the **Great Houses of Westeros**. 
*   **Aesthetic Identity:** Whether you swear fealty to the stoic, slate-grey colors of House Stark (the default styling), the crimson and gold opulence of House Lannister, or the verdant, floral aesthetics of House Tyrell, every single structural footprint of the UI bends to the active house currently running.
*   **Custom Typography:** We moved away from default system fonts, specifically injecting highly stylistic Google Web Fonts (like *Cinzel*, *Cormorant Garamond*, and *Playfair Display*) that match medieval, manuscript, and heraldic tones to make the reading experience feel like flipping through a Maester's tome.
*   **Purpose-Built UX:** It's designed to feel like you are conversing with a Grand Maester in Oldtown, seeking counsel about the known world. 

## Features

- **House Swapper:** Instantly change the entire frontend UI (colors, fonts, borders, shadows) by declaring your house allegiance in the appearance settings.
- **Lore-Accurate Aesthetics:** Painstakingly curated color palettes (OKLCH) specific to Stark, Targaryen, Lannister, Baratheon, and Tyrell.
- **Modern Stack:** Built using React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and TanStack Router/Start to ensure this heavily customized frontend remains absurdly fast and responsive. 

I built this believing that interacting with AI doesn't have to feel like staring at a terminal—it can feel like stepping into a different world.
